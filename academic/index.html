Projects

Current projects form a path from one-shot symbolic prediction, through a benchmark for classical methods and LLM agents, toward an AI researcher for equation discovery.

arXiv preprint

FoundPDE

A generative foundation model for data-driven symbolic discovery of one-dimensional time-dependent PDEs. The model maps discretized solution trajectories to symbolic PDE expressions, then refines coefficients with BFGS.

data-to-symbol Fourier features coefficient refinement
Under review

PDEDiscoveryBench

A benchmark for discovering governing equations from observations: 337 ODE/PDE systems, seven methods from sparse regression to tool-using LLM agents, three levels of disclosed scientific prior, and four noise tiers. Results depend strongly on the prior a method receives, at every noise level.

benchmarks LLM agents scientific priors
Next project

PDEScientist

An AI researcher for equation discovery that combines LLM reasoning with sparse regression, symbolic regression, and simulation, and runs the loop of choosing methods, designing experiments, and validating results. Supported by Anthropic's AI for Science Program.

agentic AI experiment design hypothesis refinement

Publications

Selected publications, manuscripts, and research documents. The newest work is centered on equation discovery and on evaluating AI agents for science.

Under review

PDEDiscoveryBench

First-author benchmark paper comparing classical equation-discovery methods and tool-using LLM agents on 337 ODE/PDE systems under controlled scientific priors and noise, with a case study on unlabeled CFD blood-flow fields.
arXiv 2026

SciExam for ENSO: Can AI Agents Build Climate Models?

AI agents build low-order stochastic models of the El Niño-Southern Oscillation from real observations, and hidden graders score them against a published reference model. Six of twelve agent-built models exceed the reference.
arXiv 2026

FoundPDE: A generative foundation model for data-driven symbolic discovery of partial differential equations

Preprint on transformer-based data-to-symbol PDE discovery from spatiotemporal solution data, with coefficient refinement, sparse-data evaluation, few-shot adaptation, and step-by-step compositional discovery.
Prospectus

Symbolic PDE Discovery with Foundation Models and Agentic Scientific Discovery
